\begin{abstract}
This paper proposes a method for estimating the optimal slip ratio for electric vehicle driving force control. 
The proposed method estimates the road surface  $\mu-\lambda$ curve in real time from partially observed data using a machine learning model and determines the optimal slip ratio from its peak.
Offline evaluation using experimental data demonstrates its effectiveness.
\end{abstract}
