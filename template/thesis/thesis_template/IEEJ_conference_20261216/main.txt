         This paper proposes a method for estimating the optimal slip ratio for electric vehicle driving force control. The method estimates
       the tire-road µ–λ curve in real time from partially observed data using a machine learning model and derives the optimal slip ratio
       from its peak. Online vehicle experiments demonstrate the eﬀectiveness of the proposed method.
